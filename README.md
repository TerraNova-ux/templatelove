# 💖 Special Website Template for Cherivelle

A beautifully crafted, modern, and deeply heartfelt romantic website dedicated to **Cherivelle**.

Designed with blush rose-gold tones, luminous glassmorphism, interactive elements, and custom tribute sections celebrating her inner strength and unmatched partnership.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **Dedicated Tribute to Her Strength & Partnership**:
   - Highlighted tribute acknowledging Cherivelle's resilience, courage under pressure, and steadfast dedication.
   - Core messages explicitly reminding her: *"You are strong and an extraordinary partner."*

2. **Wax-Sealed Handwritten Letter Modal**:
   - Interactive letter sealed with wax stamp **"C"** that opens up into an elegant parchment paper love letter with heartfelt writing and signatures.

3. **Ambient Particle Canvas Engine**:
   - Organic floating rose petals and soft sparkling starlight floating in real-time.
   - Interactive mouse/pointer movement trail that leaves stardust and sparkles.

4. **Procedural Soft Ambient Harmony (Web Audio API)**:
   - Built-in soothing ambient music box melody generated directly in the browser—no missing mp3 files, external audio dependencies, or broken links!
   - Can be turned on/off anytime with the top navigation bar, featuring an animated audio equalizer visualizer.

5. **Cherivelle's Interactive Jar of Love Notes**:
   - 15+ personalized, empowering affirmations and reminders celebrating her heart, grit, smile, and mind.
   - Click to pull a fresh love note anytime she needs a boost or comfort.

6. **3D Flip Keepsake Cards ("Endless Reasons")**:
   - Interactive 3D cards that flip on hover or tap to reveal personal tokens of admiration.

7. **Polaroid Photo Gallery**:
   - Vintage-styled Polaroid gallery with heart pins, gentle rotations, and romantic captions.

8. **Virtual Hug & Heart Explosion Generator**:
   - Tap-and-hold interactive heart button that showers the screen with bursts of flying emojis and saves the count in `localStorage`.

9. **Cherished Time Counter**:
   - Live counter tracking days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

10. **Day & Candlelight (Night Mode) Toggle**:
    - Switch between soft daylight blush and intimate midnight candlelight with persistent memory.

## 🛠️ How to Customize

- **Change Anniversary / Start Date**:
  Open [`script.js`](file:///c:/Users/vc/Desktop/main/temp1/script.js) and modify `relationshipStartDate: "YYYY-MM-DD"`.
- **Add / Edit Photos**:
  In [`index.html`](file:///c:/Users/vc/Desktop/main/temp1/index.html), search for `.polaroid-gallery` and replace the `src="..."` URLs with paths to your favorite photos together.
- **Add More Love Notes**:
  In [`script.js`](file:///c:/Users/vc/Desktop/main/temp1/script.js), simply add new strings to the `loveNotes` array inside `CHERIVELLE_CONFIG`.
